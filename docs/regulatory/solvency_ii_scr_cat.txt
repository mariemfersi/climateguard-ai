================================================================================
EIOPA SOLVENCY II DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2015/35
Sub-module: Non-life Catastrophe Risk Sub-module (Article 105 & Article 118-125)
================================================================================

Article 105 - Non-life Catastrophe Risk Sub-module Scope:
1. The non-life catastrophe risk sub-module shall reflect the risk arising from extreme or exceptional events, including natural catastrophes (windstorm, hurricane, flood, earthquake, wildfire) and man-made catastrophes.
2. The Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR) for natural catastrophe risk shall be calculated as a 1-in-200 year Value-at-Risk (VaR at 99.5% confidence level over a 1-year time horizon).

Article 105a - Natural Catastrophe Risk Calculation & Sub-modules:
1. The SCR for natural catastrophe risk shall equal the combination of the capital requirements for the following peril sub-modules:
   (a) Natural Catastrophe Windstorm risk;
   (b) Natural Catastrophe Earthquake risk;
   (c) Natural Catastrophe Flood risk;
   (d) Natural Catastrophe Hail and Severe Convective Storm risk;
   (e) Natural Catastrophe Wildfire risk.
2. Undertakings shall calculate the net loss distribution after taking into account reinsurance treaties, retrocession, and special purpose vehicles (SPVs).

Article 118 - Reinsurance & Risk Mitigation Recognition:
1. Reinsurance arrangements (Excess of Loss, Quota Share, Stop Loss) shall be recognized in the SCR calculation only where there is an effective transfer of risk.
2. Reinsurance contracts shall specify clear attachment points, aggregate limits, reinstatement clauses, and hours clauses (e.g. 72-hour clause for hurricane windstorm events).
3. Undertakings must evaluate cedent retention and potential exhaustion of layer limits under severe 1-in-100 and 1-in-200 year catastrophe scenarios.

Article 124 - Quantitative Model Governance & Validation:
1. Catastrophe models, whether internal or vendor-based (AIR, RMS), shall be subject to independent validation.
2. Climate-change non-stationarity must be explicitly assessed where historical baseline frequency/severity parameters no longer reflect forward-looking physical risk trajectories.
3. Every quantitative figure in Solvency II reporting (SFCR - Solvency and Financial Condition Report; RSR - Regular Supervisory Report) shall be fully auditable and traceable back to verified data sources.
