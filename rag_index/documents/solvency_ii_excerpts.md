## Article 101 — Solvency Capital Requirement (general)

The Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR) shall be calibrated so as to ensure that all quantifiable risks to which an insurance or reinsurance undertaking is exposed are taken into account. It shall cover existing business, as well as the new business expected to be written over the following 12 months. With respect to existing business, it shall cover only unexpected losses.

The SCR shall correspond to the Value-at-Risk of the basic own funds of an insurance or reinsurance undertaking subject to a confidence level of 99.5% over a one-year period.

## Article 105 — Non-life underwriting risk module

The non-life underwriting risk module shall reflect the risk arising from non-life insurance obligations, in relation to the perils covered and the processes used in the conduct of business.

It shall be calculated as a combination of the capital requirements for at least the following sub-modules:
- non-life premium and reserve risk
- non-life catastrophe risk
- non-life lapse risk

## Non-life catastrophe risk sub-module

The non-life catastrophe risk sub-module shall reflect the risk of loss, or of adverse change in the value of insurance liabilities, resulting from significant uncertainty of pricing and provisioning assumptions related to extreme or exceptional events.

For natural catastrophe risk the following perils shall be considered where relevant:
- windstorm
- earthquake
- flood
- hail
- subsidence

## Article 41 & 44 — System of governance and risk management (model use)

Insurance and reinsurance undertakings shall have in place an effective system of governance which provides for sound and prudent management of the business.

Where an undertaking uses a partial or full internal model, the administrative, management or supervisory body shall approve the application to use the model and any subsequent major changes. The undertaking shall ensure that the model is adequately validated and that its limitations are well understood.

Any model used for the calculation of the SCR or for pricing shall be subject to regular validation cycles and shall produce outputs that can be explained to the supervisory authority.

## Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA)

Every insurance and reinsurance undertaking shall conduct its own risk and solvency assessment as part of its risk-management system. That assessment shall include at least:
- the overall solvency needs taking into account the specific risk profile
- compliance with capital requirements
- the significance with which the risk profile of the undertaking deviates from the assumptions underlying the SCR calculation

Climate-related risks that may affect the risk profile over the medium to long term shall be considered in the ORSA forward-looking assessment.
